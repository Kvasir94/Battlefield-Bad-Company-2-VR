# SPAS carry and loading-rail correction

The headset run `native-trace-20261001-233240-651` acquired eight shells and started seven native cycles, but recorded no physical rail capture, seat, submission or completion. The user confirmed shell presentation while describing it as floaty with no discernible magnetic track. The session's streaming interruptions are a separate problem; they do not turn this reload test into a success.

Two concrete issues were found:

1. `Bc2PhysicalReload` selected `Guided` whenever the recognizer returned valid targets, including the `Free` phase. Those targets retained the correct older contact packet for gesture recognition, but made free-carried presentation use that older wrist-relative geometry. Carried presentation now remains on the actual current renderer wrist until the recognizer captures the rail. The 11,590 old “guided” pose count is not evidence of 11,590 rail captures.
2. The first SPAS profile required an observed approach farther than 3cm along the outward axial direction. A radial approach from below could enter the same valid 3cm capture sphere without ever arming. Profile revision2 explicitly enables `FrontHemisphere` approach for this single-shell item. The original native approach evidence was predominantly upward; the terminal rail itself remains a deliberate interaction candidate, not a proven native loading socket.

The capture sphere remains 3cm, release distance6cm, capture cone35°, release cone70°, maximum pose step4cm/60°, stroke5cm, alignment120ms and seating dwell60ms. Keyed orientation remains unchanged. Existing profiles default to `AxialFront`; magazines cannot enable the new single-shell approach mode. The policy still requires an observed outside approach in the front hemisphere, refuses initial-inside and back-side entry, and rejects teleporting across the capture region. No ammo credit or native acknowledgement is inferred from geometry.

The consumer now saves a ring of192 original-contact geometry rows plus exact aggregate counts. Each row records cycle/item, original/current sequence, original observation/deadline, processing time, rail-tip coordinates, radial distance, keyed/axis angle, previous logged displacement/rotation, actual insertion phase, reasons, progress and capture/seat events. The first16 cycle/item pairs also retain their nearest approach and nearest front-side approach across the entire run, so holding a shell far away before ending cannot erase the meaningful near-port evidence. Overflow samples are counted explicitly. Previous logged deltas are diagnostic comparisons rather than assertions about the recognizer's internal history after reset. The `inside_capture_geometry` field does not imply arming, native permission, or a committed insertion.

The previous headset trace did not serialize complete raw left-wrist rotations at the contact boundary. Its repeated `814` rejection means `InsertionRejected/PoseJump`, while `800` follows repeated packets after the reset. That evidence does not reveal the exact user's failed angle or distance. New metrics will resolve that uncertainty without moving the inferred port or enlarging tolerances blindly.

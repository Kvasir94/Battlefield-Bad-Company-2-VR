adapted from the documented implementation in Norbyte's LSLib:

Container member tags were cross-checked against the public GR2Lib declarations:
https://github.com/matty45/GR2Lib/blob/main/granny_formats.py
The actual Frostbite wrapper, string indices, section boundaries, relocations
and field descriptors are read and validated from the installed resources.

The compact-knot endpoint convention was checked against the author's
Granny comparison experiment, not copied as source:
https://github.com/alanjg/Heroes/blob/master/Gr2Converter/Gr2Converter.cpp
Interior spline evaluation still requires comparison with the target runtime
before claiming exact native interpolation. Static constant-curve results do
not depend on interior interpolation.
